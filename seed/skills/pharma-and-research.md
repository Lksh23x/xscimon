## Relevance scoring guide

  Score HIGH (0.7–1.0) for posts that contain any of:
  - Announcement of a new result, finding, or breakthrough ("we show", "we demonstrate", "first evidence", "surpasses", "outperforms")
  - A new model, method, architecture, or framework being introduced or released
  - A new approach, technique, or paradigm shift in the field
  - Link to a preprint, paper, or dataset (arxiv, biorxiv, Nature, Science, Cell, PNAS, ICML, NeurIPS, etc.)
  - Reference to or endorsement by a recognised authority or collaborator in the field
  - Specific quantitative claims (metrics, benchmarks, measurements)
  - Code, model weights, or tool release relevant to the domain

  Score MEDIUM (0.3–0.6) for:
  - Informed commentary or synthesis of recent work
  - Curated reading lists or thread summaries with substance
  - Conference talk or poster announcements with technical content
  - Questions or discussions that reflect genuine domain expertise

  Score LOW (0.0–0.2) for:
  - Congratulatory replies ("great work!", "congrats!", "well done", "love this")
  - Personal posts, lifestyle content, or off-topic opinions
  - Vague hype without technical substance ("this is huge", "game changer" with no details)
  - Retweets or quotes that add no original insight
  - Self-promotional posts without a concrete result or release
  - Posts that are purely social ("thanks for the follow", "happy Friday")
